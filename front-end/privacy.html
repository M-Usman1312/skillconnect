SkillConnect respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Listing information: name, phone number or email, service title, category, city/area, price, and description.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working and improve it.
  • Enquiry information: details you share when contacting a provider through the Platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To publish and display your service listing to other users;
  • To allow customers to contact you;
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud, and remove spam;
  • To improve the Platform and respond to support requests.

3. Public Information

Service listings are publicly visible. Please be aware that the name, location, and contact details you include in a listing will be seen by anyone visiting the website. Do not include information you are not comfortable sharing publicly, such as your full home address.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only: (a) with service providers that help us operate the Platform (such as hosting), under confidentiality obligations; (b) when required by law or by a valid legal request; or (c) to protect the rights, safety, or property of SkillConnect or its users.

5. Data Retention

We keep listing data for as long as the listing is active. You may ask us to remove your listing at any time, and we will delete it within a reasonable period, subject to legal requirements.

6. Cookies

The Platform may use essential cookies or local storage to function properly. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for children under 18 without parental consent. If we learn that we have collected information from a child without consent, we will delete it.
